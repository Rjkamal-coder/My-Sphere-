Privacy Policy

How My Daily Routine handles your information.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

My Daily Routine is a personal productivity application that helps you record routines, prayers, expenses, trading activity, tasks and profile information. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide an email address, account profile details, profile photo, prayer records, expense entries, trading journal entries, calendar tasks and app preferences. Please do not enter passwords for other services, payment-card details or information you do not want stored.

2. How information is used

Your information is used to create and secure your account and to provide the app's features, display your own records, calculate summaries and remember preferences. The app does not sell your personal information.

3. Storage and service providers

Account authentication and app data are processed through the Supabase project configured for this website. Profile photos are stored in its avatar storage service. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to their services; their own privacy policies apply.

4. Who can see your information

App records are intended to be private to your account and protected by the database access policies configured for this project. Profile photo links may be publicly readable so that profile images can display. Do not upload a photo that you want to keep strictly private. Authorized administrators may access limited platform-level information as permitted by the app's security configuration.

5. Security

The app uses Supabase authentication and database access policies. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password, sign out on shared devices and keep your login details private.

6. Your choices and data requests

You can update profile details and manage or delete many records through the app. To request help with account or personal-data deletion, contact us using the WhatsApp or Telegram links in the footer and include the email address associated with your account. Never send your password. Some information may remain in backups or be retained where required for security or legal reasons.

7. Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through WhatsApp or Telegram.
