Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Growtica provides personal organization tools for routines, prayer tracking, expenses, trading journals, tasks and market news. This policy explains, at a high level, how information is handled when you use the service.

Information you provide

When you create an account, the service processes your email address and account credentials through Supabase Authentication. Information you choose to save in your workspace, such as profile details, expenses, prayer records, trades and tasks, is stored to provide the features you request.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, operate your workspace, display your saved records, maintain account settings and support application security. User records are intended to be protected by Supabase row-level security policies.

Service providers and storage

Growtica uses Supabase for authentication, database and related storage services. Your browser also stores a theme preference and may cache application files for offline shell access. The application should not cache authenticated database responses in its service worker.

News and financial information

News content and trading journal features are informational and organizational tools. They are not personalized investment advice, and no trading result is guaranteed.

Advertising

The current project files do not include an advertising integration. If advertising such as Google AdSense is enabled later, this policy and the site consent experience should be reviewed and updated to explain advertising cookies, personalization choices and relevant third-party processing before ads are served.

Your choices

You can stop using the service and contact the team through the official community links on the homepage. Account or data deletion requests should be made through the service operator. Do not submit passwords or sensitive credentials through public contact channels.

Contact

Use the WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook links provided on the Growtica homepage to contact the service team.

This policy should be reviewed and updated by the site operator to reflect the actual deployed configuration, applicable laws, analytics, advertising and retention practices before production launch.
