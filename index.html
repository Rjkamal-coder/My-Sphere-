ABOUT GROWTICA

A clearer view of your everyday progress.

Growtica is a web-based personal organization platform designed to bring useful everyday tools into one convenient workspace. Track routines, prayers, expenses, trading activity and tasks while keeping your information connected to your own account.

Your workspace is designed for personal organization and reflection. Market coverage is informational and does not constitute personalized investment advice or guarantee trading results.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is Growtica free to use?

You can create an account at no cost. Any future paid features or changes will be clearly communicated.

Can I use Growtica on my phone?

Yes. Growtica is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Does Growtica provide financial or investment advice?

No. News and trading journal tools are for information and personal record-keeping, not personalized financial advice.

How can I contact Growtica?

Use the contact links below to reach the Growtica team.

Is my workspace private?

Account data is associated with your signed-in account and protected by the application's Supabase access policies.
